Google Business Profile (formerly known as Google My Business or GMB) is a free tool provided by Google that allows businesses to manage their presence across Google Search and Google Maps. When customers search for businesses like yours on Google, an optimized profile helps you stand out and attract calls, website clicks, and foot traffic.

Over 80% of local consumers search for nearby services and products online. Having an optimized, verified Google Business Profile places your business directly into Google's Local 3-Pack and Maps, driving real phone calls, direction requests, customer reviews, and high-intent leads.

Depending on Google's assigned verification method (Video Verification, Email, Phone/SMS, or Postcard), the process can take anywhere from 24 to 72 hours for digital methods, up to 1-2 weeks if manual Google review is required.
